FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about going solar with BBN Solutions.

How much does a solar system cost in Gurugram/Haryana?

+

A 3 kW residential system typically costs ₹1.5 – ₹2.2 lakh after subsidies. A 10 kW commercial system ranges from ₹4.5 – ₹6.5 lakh. With central government subsidies (up to 40% for residential) and HAREDA state subsidies, the effective cost is significantly lower. Contact us for an exact quote tailored to your property.

Am I eligible for government solar subsidy in Haryana?

+

Yes! Under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residential consumers can get up to 40% subsidy on systems up to 3 kW, and 20% for 3–10 kW. Commercial and industrial customers can avail accelerated depreciation benefits. BBN Solutions handles all documentation and applications free of charge.

How long does solar installation take?

+

A residential system (3–10 kW) is typically installed in 1–3 days. Larger commercial or industrial systems (25 kW+) may take 5–15 days. After installation, net meter approval from DHBVN/UHBVN takes 10–30 days. Our team coordinates with the utility for seamless, on-time grid connection.

What warranties and guarantees does BBN Solutions provide?

+

We provide: 25-year performance warranty on solar panels (90% output till year 12, 80% till year 25), 5-year product warranty on panels, 5-year warranty on inverters, 10-year warranty on lithium batteries, and 5 years of free AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) on all installations.

Can I get a solar loan or EMI option?

+

Absolutely! BBN Solutions is tied up with HDFC Bank, SBI, ICICI, and several NBFCs for solar loans at attractive interest rates (7–9% p.a.). Zero-cost EMI options are also available. With typical savings exceeding your EMI from month one, you go solar at no extra monthly cost.

Does solar work on cloudy or rainy days in Haryana?

+

Yes! Modern solar panels generate electricity even on cloudy days — typically 10–30% of rated output. Haryana has over 300 sunny days per year, making it one of India's best regions for solar. Our system design accounts for local weather patterns to maximise annual energy generation.

What is the payback period and ROI?

+

For a typical residential system in Haryana, the payback period is 3–5 years. After that, you enjoy free electricity for 20+ years. The overall ROI is 20–25% per year — far better than any fixed deposit or mutual fund. Commercial and industrial systems often have even shorter payback periods.

Do I need to maintain solar panels? How often?

+

Solar panels require minimal maintenance. A quarterly cleaning (to remove dust and bird droppings) maintains peak efficiency. BBN Solutions' AMC plans include scheduled cleaning, electrical checks, inverter health monitoring, and emergency call-outs — starting at just ₹3,500/year for residential systems.
